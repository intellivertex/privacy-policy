Privacy Policy

Privacy contact: intellivertex@gmail.com

Important: This Privacy Policy describes IntelliVertex's current general privacy practices. We may update it when our services, applications, or applicable legal requirements change.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how apps published by IntelliVertex ("we", "us") handle information. It is meant to describe our actual practices. Where a feature or service is described as conditional, it applies only when an application uses that feature or service.

2. Scope

This policy applies to mobile applications and games published by IntelliVertex that link to it. Individual apps may have different features and data practices.

3. Information We Collect

A category below applies only when the relevant app actually uses it.

A. Information stored locally on the device

Data the app saves on your device, such as settings or progress (see section 4). Unless an app states otherwise, we do not receive this data.

B. Information collected automatically

Some services built into an app may automatically process technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, IP address, and interaction or diagnostic data. Which of these are processed depends on the services the app uses.

C. Information provided voluntarily

Information you choose to send us, for example by emailing us.

D. Advertising-related information

Applies only to apps that show ads. See section 5.

E. Analytics and crash information

Applies only to apps that use analytics or crash reporting. See sections 7 and 8.

F. Information collected through third-party services

Third-party SDKs and services included in an app may process information under their own policies. See section 9.

Our applications do not currently request access to your device location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

4. Information That May Be Stored Locally

Depending on the app, data stored on your device may include app settings, game progress, scores, achievements, unlocked content, preferences, language selection, audio settings, customization choices, and locally stored identifiers. Not every app stores every item.

Local storage versus transmission. Data stored only on your device stays under your control and is not sent to us or to a server unless the app has a feature that transmits it. Third-party SDKs in an app may separately store their own identifiers or preferences on the device, such as consent choices. An app that sends data to a server or cloud service will describe that processing in this Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

Our applications may display advertising, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Advertising is provided through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may process information such as device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising identifiers, app interaction information, and diagnostic information. Not all of this information is necessarily collected in every case.

Apps that contain ads may use advertising SDKs, such as Google AdMob, to request and display ads, measure ad performance, and help prevent fraud. Advertising SDKs may process information such as device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising identifiers, app interaction information, and diagnostic information. Not all of this is necessarily collected in every case.

The exact processing depends on the advertising provider, the app's configuration, your choices, your region, and applicable consent requirements. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on these factors. Apps without ads do not use advertising SDKs.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

Our applications use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy and consent choices.

Where an app uses Google UMP, you may be shown a privacy or consent message depending on your location and applicable requirements. Your selections are used to configure how advertising services handle personal information in that app. A consent choice applies to the purposes it describes; it does not mean you agreed to all data collection or to any processing not described to you.

Where the app provides a Privacy Settings option, you may be able to review or change your choices there. You can also reset your device's advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your Android settings.

7. Analytics

If an application uses analytics, the analytics service may process usage and diagnostic information such as screens or features used, session information, device and app details, and identifiers, to help us understand how the app is used. Applications that do not use an analytics service do not process information through an analytics service.

8. Crash Reporting

IntelliVertex does not currently use a dedicated third-party crash-reporting service in its applications. If this changes, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

9. Third-Party Services

Only services actually used by our applications apply.

10. How We Use Information

Depending on the app, information is used to: operate the app; save progress and settings; provide features you request; display advertisements; measure app performance; diagnose crashes; improve functionality; prevent abuse and fraud; and comply with legal obligations. A purpose applies only if the app has the related feature.

In the future, some applications may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing or another supported platform. If introduced, the relevant payment processing will be handled by the applicable platform, and this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

11. Data Sharing

  • Stored locally: not shared by us, unless a feature you use transmits it.
  • Third-party service providers: SDKs included in an app, such as advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, may receive information as described in section 9. They process it under their own terms and policies.
  • Advertising-related processing: ad providers and their partners may process information to serve and measure ads, subject to your choices where available.
  • Legal disclosures: we may disclose information where required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

IntelliVertex does not sell personal information.

We do not claim that no data is shared where third-party SDKs are present.

12. Data Retention

Retention depends on the data and where it is held. Data stored locally remains until you clear it, reset the app, or uninstall it (subject to Android backup behavior). Data held by third-party services is retained under their policies.

13. Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, using any in-app reset option, or uninstalling the app. Some data may remain in device backups managed by your Google account.

For data held by third-party services, see their policies or contact us at the address in section 20 and we will tell you what we can and cannot do. We do not promise server-side deletion for apps with no server or account system.

14. Children's Privacy

Apps intended for children, or that mix audiences, may be subject to additional requirements, including restrictions on advertising and data collection. We will apply any additional requirements that are relevant to an application and its intended audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a way that is not permitted, contact us and we will review it.

15. Security

We take reasonable measures, appropriate to the app, to protect information, such as using HTTPS for network connections where applicable and relying on Android's app sandboxing for local data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. International Data Processing

Third-party services used by an app may process information in countries other than your own. Where an app transfers personal data across borders, relevant safeguards depend on the provider and applicable law.

17. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to or restrict processing of, and port your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. These rights are not available in all situations. Where we hold no personal information that identifies you, we may be unable to action a request. To make a request, use section 20.

18. Regional Privacy Information

European Economic Area / United Kingdom

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the controller is IntelliVertex. . You may have the rights in section 17 and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

California

Where the CCPA/CPRA applies to us, California residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Other US states

Some other states provide similar rights, such as access, deletion, correction and opt-out of targeted advertising, subject to thresholds and exemptions that may not apply to us.

Other jurisdictions

Other countries may provide additional rights. We will respond to requests as required by the law that applies to us.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as our apps or the law change. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes, for example by updating this page or in the app.
